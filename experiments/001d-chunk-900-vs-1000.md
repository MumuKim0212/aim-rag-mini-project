# 001d: 900 vs 1000 on the final chain

Status: decided (stays 900/112; 1000 is higher on the answer score but inside the noise)
Date: 2026-10-21

## 1. Hypothesis

- Change (one variable): `chunking.chunk_size` 900 → 1000 (overlap 112 → 125) in the final RAG chain (`configs/007-flash36.yaml`: structure units for glossary and terms, Recursive for the other 7 documents, hybrid k=5, `gemini-3.6-flash`). Variant `configs/001d-chunk1000-chain.yaml`. Requested by the project owner on 2026-10-21 ("which is higher, 900 or 1000").
- Why a new run: cards 001b/001c compared 900 and 1000 on the early vector chain with Recursive chunking for all documents (`evid@5` 0.771 vs 0.759). In the final chain the size only applies to the documents outside the glossary and terms, and retrieval and the answer model differ. Rerunning the old retrieval-only comparison would give identical numbers (a fixed index and fixed questions are deterministic).
- Expected effect: none predicted; the earlier difference was 0.012 `evid@5`, a few questions.

## 2. Measurement

- Baseline run (reused): `dev_007_flash36` (900/112 chain: answers and grades) and `dev_004_hybrid_retrieval` for retrieval metrics of the same chunking and retrieval (its model differs only in the answer step, which retrieval does not use).
- Retrieval-only run of the variant, then one answer + judge run. Split: dev, 90 questions (74 with gold pages). Noise: 14 of 90 changed in a rerun, net −8.
- Not looked at: test split, self-written golden set.

## 3. Go/no-go criteria (written before results)

- Which is higher: reported as the two `evid@5` means (74 questions) and the two answer scores (90 questions), with the paired answer comparison.
- The final chain moves to 1000/125 only if the answer run satisfies: paired net wins ≥ 9, more than 14 questions changed, 95% CI of the score difference excludes 0, context ≤ 1.3× the 900 chain's characters. Otherwise it stays 900/112, whatever the point estimates are.
- A difference inside the noise is described as "not distinguishable", not as a win.

## 4. LLM budget

- Index and retrieval-only run: embeddings only (about 0.14 USD). Answer run: answers 90 + judge 90 (about 0.36 USD).
- Calls so far in the project: 2,280 → 2,460 after this card.

## 6. Decision and notes

- Decision: **the final chain stays 900/112.** 1000 does not meet the section 3 conditions (net +4 vs 9 required, 14 changed vs more than 14, CI includes 0).
- Answer to the question "which is higher": retrieval `evid@5` is higher with 900 (0.853 vs 0.835), the answer score is higher with 1000 (0.844 vs 0.822). Neither difference can be told from noise: the retrieval difference is 5 questions each way, and the answer difference is net +4 against a rerun noise of 14 changed questions (net −8 in the one rerun). The two measures point in opposite directions, which is what noise looks like; no claim is made that either size is better.
- Why 900 stays: the pre-registered rule needs evidence beyond noise to change the chain, and the earlier comparison (cards 001b, 001c) also favoured 900 at retrieval level. The 1000 answer-level point estimate is the only thing in favour of 1000.
