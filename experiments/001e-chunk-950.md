# 001e: 950 on the final chain

Status: planned
Date: 2026-10-21

## 1. Hypothesis

- Change (one variable): `chunking.chunk_size` 900 → 950 (overlap 112 → 119, size/8 rounded) in the final RAG chain, as in card 001d. Variant `configs/001e-chunk950-chain.yaml`. Requested by the project owner on 2026-10-21 after card 001d.
- Known before this card: on the final chain 900 and 1000 are not distinguishable (card 001d: `evid@5` 0.853 vs 0.835, answer score 0.822 vs 0.844, net +4, inside noise). 950 lies between them; no effect is predicted, and a difference from 900 smaller than the noise is the likely outcome.
- This is a third size next to 900 and 1000 on the same 74/90 dev questions; whichever looks best is partly a chance pick (cards 001b, 001c). The rule below therefore keeps 900 unless the evidence is beyond noise.

## 2. Measurement

- Baseline run (reused): `dev_007_flash36` (900/112 chain: answers and grades) and `dev_004_hybrid_retrieval` (its retrieval metrics). Also shown for context: 1000 (`dev_001d_chunk1000chain*`).
- Retrieval-only run, then one answer + judge run of 950. Split: dev, 90 questions (74 with gold pages). Noise: 14 of 90 changed in a rerun, net −8.
- Not looked at: test split, self-written golden set.

## 3. Go/no-go criteria (written before results)

- Which is higher: reported (retrieval `evid@5` and answer score for 900, 950, 1000).
- The chain moves to 950/119 only if the answer run against `dev_007_flash36` gives: paired net wins ≥ 9, more than 14 questions changed, 95% CI of the score difference excludes 0, context ≤ 1.3× the 900 chain's characters. Otherwise it stays 900/112.
- A difference inside the noise is called "not distinguishable". No further sizes are tested after this card.

## 4. LLM budget

- Index and retrieval-only run: embeddings only (about 0.14 USD). Answer run: answers 90 + judge 90 (about 0.36 USD).
